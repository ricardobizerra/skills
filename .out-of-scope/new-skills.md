# Out of Scope: Redundant or Non-Engineering New Skills

**Status:** Rejected (standing decision)

## What Is Rejected

Proposals for new skills that:
- Can already be composed from existing skills in `skills/engineering/` (use `/ask-rblf` to check composition first).
- Target non-engineering domains (personal productivity, content writing, marketing, general brainstorming).
- Address a one-off personal preference rather than a recurring, observed engineering failure.

## Why

Every skill increases surface area, maintenance burden, and routing ambiguity. New skills are added only when a recurring engineering bottleneck cannot be cleanly solved by existing playbooks.
